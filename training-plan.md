## Program Philosophy

This plan is built on research-backed principles from PRVN Fitness (James Kelly, 3rd at 2024 World Champs) and HAOS (Hunter McIntyre, multiple World Champion):

1. **Running is the race** — a 2025 *Frontiers in Physiology* study confirmed runs account for ~60% of total race time and VO2max is the single strongest predictor of finish time. We build the aerobic engine first.
2. **Compromised running is the real skill** — blood lactate spikes highest *during stations*, then you immediately have to run. Every Saturday session trains you to clear lactate and hold pace after a station, not before.
3. **Station efficiency over raw fitness** — each station has a sustainable pace. Muscular endurance and movement efficiency matter far more than one-rep-max strength. The goal is to find your station pace in training so you never blow up.
4. **70/30 conditioning-to-strength split** — HAOS methodology: aerobic and conditioning work is primary; strength is a foundation that enables station performance, not the training focus.

- Dynamic drills: leg swings (front/back, lateral) × 10 each, hip circles × 10, A-skips × 2×20m, butt kicks × 2×20m

**Main (30 min)**

### Saturday 5 Jul — RACE DAY 🏆

**Morning protocol:**
- Wake 2.5–3 hrs before race start
- Breakfast: something you've tested before (oats + banana, or toast + eggs)
- Arrive at venue 60 min before start
- Warm-up: 10 min easy jog → dynamic drills → 2 × 100m light burpee broad jumps → 2 × SkiErg 150m

**Race strategy — Open Male, 5:30/km target:**
- Runs 1–3: Hold back. Your adrenaline will want you to go faster. Trust the pace. You should feel like you're leaving something in the tank.
- SkiErg: Long arms, full hinge, 4:00–4:30 total — don't sprint it
- Sled Push: Stay low, small fast steps, lock your hips
- Sled Pull: Hand-over-hand, don't yank — smooth rhythm wins
- Burpee Broad Jumps: Breathe on the way up, jump for distance not height, aim ~5–6 sec per rep
- Row: 1:58–2:05/500m split — hold this no matter what
- Farmers Carry: Grip the handles tight, walk fast, change hands if needed at 100m
- Sandbag Lunges: Deep lunge, drive through front heel, keep sandbag on shoulders
- Wall Balls: Establish a rhythm (e.g., 25-20-15-20-20 or unbroken) — squat below parallel every rep
- Runs 5–8: This is where the race is won. If you've been disciplined, you'll have gas here.

**Enjoy it. You've earned it.**

## Key Rules Throughout

1. **No running through knee pain.** Mild fatigue is fine. Sharp, localised pain is a stop signal.
2. **Every Monday run is non-negotiable.** That's your primary fitness stimulus.
3. **Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE):** Easy = 5–6/10. Tempo = 7–8/10. Intervals = 8–9/10. Race day = 9/10.
4. **Sleep and nutrition matter as much as the training.** Aim 7–8 hrs/night. Eat enough protein (1.6–2g/kg = ~120–150g/day).
5. **Record every session.** Pace, RPE, how the knee felt. Adjust the following week accordingly.
